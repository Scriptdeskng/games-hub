# Credits: `naija-whot` (Naija Whot)

All art is **made in-repo by the asset-designer (Claude)** from basic shapes and paths. Licence: project-owned (same as the repository). No traced, copied or downloaded artwork; no real logos, brands, decks or people. The card layout is original (stroke-drawn digits, shape symbols, aso-oke edge strips); it does not reproduce any commercial Whot deck. Generator: `scripts/gen-naija-whot-art.mjs` (one generator, all art); checker: `scripts/check-naija-whot-art.mjs`. SVGs are emitted compact (no svgo run: it is not installed in the repo and the generator output has no editor metadata).
