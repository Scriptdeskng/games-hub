# Akọni & Dike brief, part 3: chapter reference boards (Ekumeku)

Tags as in part 2. Palette `theme-akd-ekumeku` (forest green, uli ochre, night blue). Pack ids `ch10` (prologue) to `ch16`. Campaign mood: stealth and ambush; the opponent is **the Company and its constabulary as a system**: generic period uniforms, **no insignia, no flag, no logo, no ethnic label, ever** `[SC guardrail 3]` `[AB]` `[H-13]`. **No Igbo-Ukwu bronzes** (about 1,000 years older than the period) and no Ife art here `[AB]`. No shrines, ofo staffs, mbari houses, masquerades, war charms or nsibidi `[AB]`. Secrecy is shown by silence and cover, never by ritual objects. The codex says honestly that the Company won `[SC 7]`.

Global Ekumeku rules: sight cones use the three shape states (part 1, 2.6); burning is **never shown as flame**, only smoke `[SC]`; arrests are off-screen; no restraint imagery `[H-22]` `[D]`; the "Ekumeku" word is spoken only in a whisper bark (part 5) because the hero's line is "We do not speak of it" `[SC]`.
