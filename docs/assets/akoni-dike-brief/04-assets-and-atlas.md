# Akọni & Dike brief, part 4: assets, packs, budgets and sprite atlas plan

Root: `games/akoni-dike/assets/` (abbreviated `A/`). Maker column: **repo** = asset-designer makes it as SVG/CSS/JSON; **repo+human** = in-repo placeholder at final dimensions, a human replaces it (spec in part 7). Sizes are SVG units unless noted; budgets are gzip. SVGs go through `npx svgo`; raster is `.webp` at 2x DPR max `[CLAUDE.md]`. ## 4. Sprite atlas plan (Canvas 2D)

1. **Authoring.** SVG sheets with named groups and a `*.json` frame map generated by a repo script; consistent bottom-centre anchor and a 1 px transparent gutter.
2. **Load.** On pack start, fetch the sheet SVG, draw once into an `OffscreenCanvas` (or `createImageBitmap`) at `min(devicePixelRatio, 2)` and cache per sheet. **Never rasterise per frame.** If decode on a throttled device exceeds 600 ms per campaign, switch that sheet to a prebaked `.webp` from `scripts/export-png.mjs` (still within pack budget). Decide after the A1 vertical slice (open question 6).
3. **Draw order.** ground tiles (pre-baked chunk canvases), props behind, shadows, ally rings and markers, characters (y-sorted), projectiles and fx, props in front (canopy, awnings), sight cones, HUD. Cull outside the camera; draw each sheet in batches.
4. **Pooling.** Pool hit sparks, dust puffs, projectiles (arrows, rifle ticks), floating labels and sound-ring waves; cap 48 live fx; no per-frame allocation.
5. **Hit flash.** Draw the frame into a small pooled scratch canvas, fill cream with `source-atop`, draw it back. No `ctx.filter`.
6. **Colourways.** The rival accent is applied by tinting the white `*-accent.svg` mask once into a cached OffscreenCanvas per sheet. Ally rings are drawn, not baked.
7. **View scale (360 x 640).** Hero about 40 CSS px tall, rivals about 32, tile 32. Portrait viewport about 12 x 20 tiles; wider in landscape.
8. **Memory.** Unload the previous chapter's tiles, props and fx when a pack starts; keep `shared-<campaign>` resident; stay under 24 MB decoded.

## 5. Requests to the senior-developer (the asset-designer does not edit `src/rules` or `src/ai`)

1. Accept the pack ids and folder layout (section 1) and the pack manifest fields; make `ship.json` list `packs/*/pack.json` and conditionally exclude `packs/ch00` when `features.moremiPrologue` is off.
2. Provide a frame-map loader for the `*.json` atlas format and a pooled hit-flash helper.
3. Extend `npm run size` to report each `packs/<id>` (<= 1 MB) and the initial download (<= 3 MB).
4. Register the three theme classes from `akd-themes.css`; keep all text in `t()`; no art contains text except placeholders.
5. Wire `scripts/check-akoni-dike-art.mjs` (written by the asset-designer in A1) into `npm run check`.
