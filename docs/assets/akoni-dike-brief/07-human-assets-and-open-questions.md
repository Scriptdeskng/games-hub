## 4. Open questions for the human and the historian

1. **Moremi prologue.** Keep it or open straight on Kiriji? Ugbo remembers Moremi as a traitor `[SC]`. If kept, who reviews it from the Ugbo and Ilaje side?
2. **Chapter count.** SCRIPT.md defines 14 units. The request mentioned 15. Is there a unit missing from the source doc?
3. **Raffia raiders.** Is a visible-face, mask-free, crest-free raffia cape enough to avoid reading as masquerade regalia? A cultural reviewer decides.
4. **Firebrand duel.** `[SC]` says the firebrand "burns his cloak"; the checklist says no flames on people. Proposed resolution: only the hem scorches (ember edge, smoke), the cloak is pulled off and falls, and flame renders only on the torch head and the fallen cloak. Please confirm.
5. **Pack budget reading.** Does "each chapter pack <= 1 MB" include that chapter's audio and JSON? This brief keeps art at <= 750 KB (800 KB for ch14, which carries two branch variants; the line items sum to 799 KB there) and uses separate `audio-<campaign>` packs, so both readings pass.
6. **SVG at runtime or pre-baked `.webp` atlases?** Decide after the A1 slice with a real low-end Android (Tecno, Infinix, Itel) measurement of decode time and memory.
7. **Music length versus size.** `MUSIC_BRIEF.md` asked 30 to 60 s loops at 96 kbps, which is 360 to 720 KB and cannot meet <= 300 KB (the maximum is about 25 s at 96 kbps, about 38 s at 64 kbps). Its rules (lines 10 and 11) are now corrected, and its 45 s and 60 s slots are flagged. **Decision for the human:** shorten those shared slots to <= 38 s at 64 kbps, or accept lower bitrates (about 53 kbps for 45 s, about 40 kbps for 60 s), or raise the 300 KB limit. This brief uses 24 to 33 s at <= 64 kbps.
8. **Okpu agụ cap.** SCRIPT lists the "okpu agu cap" as an unlockable. If the cap is associated with title or rank, it should not be offered to an 18-year-old. Historian and native reviewer to decide `[H-18]`.
9. **Captain Harrow and the Company's constabulary.** How should his face and the constables be drawn without any ethnic label, and should carriers appear in the column? Until decided, Harrow's portrait is a uniform silhouette.
10. **Heritage bodies.** Which bodies or descendants' groups sign off the five likenesses, and what sources describe them? (The asset-designer cannot contact them.)
11. **Health representation.** The HUD uses a "vigour" strip, not hearts. Is a different word or icon preferred?
12. **Akin's first appearance.** SCRIPT introduces him in ch03; his sheet sits in `shared-kiriji`. Does he join the squad earlier?
13. **Whispered "Ekumeku".** ASSET_BIBLE lists it as a bark, SCRIPT says "We do not speak of it". Is a whisper-only bark acceptable?
14. **Campaign sound sets.** Is the Yoruba set (talking drum, agogo, shekere) for Kiriji and the Igbo set (ogene, udu, ekwe) for Ekumeku accurate and respectful, with no ogene in Kiriji cues?
15. **Horns.** `warHorn` (Kiriji, exception X2) and `bugle` (the Company, exception X1) are design stand-ins; the historian should say what signals were really used `[H-09]`, `[H-13]`, and a musician judges both (row 13).
16. **Latoosa.** Guardrail 4 lists him as a brief cameo, but SCRIPT only gives a codex line in ch05. Where, if anywhere, does he appear on screen? **His portrait (and his share of the heritage-body check) is conditional on the answer**: no on-screen role means a text-only codex card and no portrait.
17. **Source-card objects.** Only the Saro rifle, treaty stone and ogene are named. Who chooses the other 11, and from which museum collections (no photographs in game)?
18. **Regional balance.** The history is Yoruba and Igbo by design; the museum class carries other regions. Is that enough for Principle 3, or should later packs add other episodes?
19. **Title.** Native speakers to confirm the pairing "Akọni & Dike" (Yoruba and Igbo for brave person or hero) reads as intended.
20. **Teacher guides.** The brief only provides the cover tile; the guides and PDFs are a content deliverable for A2 and A3.
21. **Compliance.** Is weapon imagery acceptable to the telco (row 15)?
22. **Map.** Keep the modern-outline map, or drop it if no cartographer is available?
23. **Origin of the war's name (`[H-31]`).** `05` and the boards no longer say the war's name comes from the sound of the rifles; nothing in `SCRIPT.md`, `LEARNING.md` or `ASSET_BIBLE.md` supports it and the war is generally named after the place. **`LEARNING.md` line 41 (the Kiriji 3 lesson row: "how breech-loading rifles changed the war and gave it its name") uses the same phrase.** That file is the owner's and was not edited; the owner and the historian must decide the wording (and whether the "ki-ri-ji" title card has any documented basis). Until then the title card and crack are a stylised effect only.
24. **Developer open questions.** `docs/games/akoni-dike/ARCHITECTURE.md` section 16 ("Questions and contradictions for the project owner") holds the developer's list; it is not repeated or edited here. Items that overlap this brief (pack budgets, music size, SVG versus `.webp`, the chapter count, flags) should be answered once, in one place.
25. **Hausa and Edo words.** `Madalla` and `Kóyo` are dropped from this game's results pool (part 5 section 6). Does the owner want the shared celebratory pool back, with native Hausa and Edo review tags?
26. **Two-sides cards.** Decided here: included in Kiriji 1, 5, 6 and Ekumeku 1, 5, 6 (and the Moremi prologue), per the `LEARNING.md` rule "chapters 1, 5 and the finales". The Ekumeku cards pair Company records with Anioma oral history and need the historian's sources.
