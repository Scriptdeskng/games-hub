### Probes (scratch, deleted after the run)
- Rules probe, 26 tests, all pass after I fixed four wrong expectations of my own (not engine defects; I checked each against the engine by hand):
  - Skip rule at exactly 12 seeds (human and CPU), at 11, toggle off, 18 seeds from CPU pit 6 wrapping across the boundary (captures pits 1 and 0 for 4 seeds), 24 seeds never visit origin.
  - Chain stops at a 4-seed pit, chain 11..6 from human pit 5 stops at own pit 5, chain never leaves opponent row, threeOnly takes a chain of 3s (3,3,3).
  - Last seed in own row captures nothing.
  - Must-feed with exactly one feeding move (only pit 4 legal, game continues); no feeding move ends the game as stuck with owners sweep; mustFeed off allows non-reaching pits.
  - Capturer toggle when the last move captured nothing (gained 0, sweep to the non-stuck player). Sweep decides the game at 25. Cap at exactly 400 plies (399 playing, 400th ends).
- Fuzz: 32 toggle combinations x 40 seeded games = 1,280 games, 242,432 random legal moves.
